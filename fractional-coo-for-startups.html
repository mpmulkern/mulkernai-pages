What Is a Fractional COO for Startups?

Startups often face the challenge of scaling operations smartly while managing limited resources. A fractional Chief Operating Officer (COO) for startups traditionally offers part-time executive leadership to optimize business processes, improve efficiency, and drive growth without the expense of hiring a full-time C-suite executive.

Mulkern AI Systems revolutionizes this concept by providing an AI-powered fractional COO — a software agent that delivers the same strategic operational leadership, insights, and execution capabilities at a fraction of the cost and with unmatched speed and consistency.
