Fractional CTO For E-Commerce: Board-Ready Tech Leadership at AI Speed

Empower your online retail business with Mulkern AI Systems’ AI-powered fractional CTO agent — delivering strategic technology leadership, rapid innovation, and operational excellence without hiring overhead.

By Michael Mulkern, Founder & CEO

What Is a Fractional CTO for E-Commerce?

Traditional fractional CTOs provide part-time leadership to e-commerce businesses, but they often come with limited availability, high hourly costs, and delays in decision-making. Mulkern AI Systems revolutionizes this model by offering a fully AI-powered fractional CTO — a software agent equipped with advanced machine learning, domain-specific expertise, and real-time analytics tailored specifically for e-commerce technology needs.

This AI fractional CTO acts as your continuous technology strategist, product innovator, and operations overseer, ensuring your platform stays competitive, scalable, and secure without the overhead of a full-time executive.

How Mulkern AI’s Fractional CTO Transforms E-Commerce

You describe your technology stack, customer data picture and market context in a structured intake form, and the agent returns board-ready insights and actionable strategies. It is a web app you drive yourself — nothing connects to your systems — covering areas such as personalisation, fulfilment automation and fraud detection.

Unlike a human fractional CTO juggling several clients and limited hours, the agent is available whenever you need it and can be run as often as you like — each run producing a fresh deliverable from the context you supply.
