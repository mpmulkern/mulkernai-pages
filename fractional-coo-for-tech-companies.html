What Is a Fractional COO for Tech Companies?

A fractional Chief Operating Officer (COO) is a strategic executive who manages day-to-day operations, optimizes workflows, and drives organizational growth on a part-time or on-demand basis. For tech companies, this role is vital to scaling effectively without the overhead of a full-time executive.
